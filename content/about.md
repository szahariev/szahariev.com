Most of the work behind a cloud happens in software its users never see. The
control plane handles permissions, workload placement, and recovery when things
fail. That's the part I work on.

I started [Soverita](https://soverita.io) to build a European sovereign-cloud
governance platform for organizations running their own infrastructure. Most
of my time goes into designing APIs between Kubernetes and the underlying
infrastructure, and making sure operations behave correctly when something
fails or gets retried.

I've spent over twenty years building distributed systems, from carrier-grade
telephony to fintech and AI platforms. I still write code, deploy it, and debug
things when they break. I've never been particularly interested in architecture
that only works on paper.

I write and speak about distributed systems, failure handling, and the gap between
how infrastructure is designed and how it actually behaves in production.

I am based in Sofia, Bulgaria. You can reach me at
[szahariev@szahariev.com](mailto:szahariev@szahariev.com) for speaking invitations
or technical conversations.

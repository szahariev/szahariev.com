## Soverita

I founded [Soverita](https://soverita.io) to build a European
sovereign-cloud governance platform. It's for organizations that run their
own infrastructure.

Most of my work is on the control plane. That includes defining API contracts
between Kubernetes and OpenStack, dealing with provider calls that time out
without a clear result, and deciding which components get access to which keys.

A lot of the engineering comes down to getting these systems to work together
without taking control away from the people operating them.
